About This Tool

This website was created because a handful of goobers on BlockDisplayEngine decided it would be a truly brilliant idea to paint partially-empty/transparent second-layers on heads, and then scale one of the X/Y/Z values to between 10,000 and 40,000. This achieves very nice and highly detailed model pieces, at the cost of an absurd line of a display rendering several hundred blocks away from the model.

The worst abuse of this I have seen yet is 299484 "Medieval Ship", of which more than 65% (118 of 179) abuses this, resulting in a veritable army of glitchy lines rendered a few hundred or so blocks away from the model.

Now I have to check every single model I use before pasting it in, because some people are absolute farm animals and cannot be trusted with anything.
